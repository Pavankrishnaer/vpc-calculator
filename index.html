Assign Subnets to Availability Zones

In AWS, every subnet lives in exactly one AZ. AZs are physically separate data centres in a region (e.g. us-east-1a, us-east-1b in the US East region). Spreading subnets across multiple AZs means your app stays up even if one data centre has an outage. This is called high availability.

Each AZ gets its own separate CIDR block carved from the VPC. The same subnet in 2 AZs uses 2x the IPs.
All subnets are assigned to all AZs by default. Click a chip to remove it from that AZ.
